Kitchen Basics

  • Read the entire recipe from start to finish before you begin cooking.
  • Measure and prepare all ingredients before turning on the stove.
  • Keep your workspace clean and wash fresh vegetables thoroughly.
  • Store spices in a cool, dry place to keep their flavor fresh.

Basic Cooking Rules

  1. Bring water to a full boil and add a pinch of salt before adding pasta.
  2. Preheat the oven for 10 minutes before placing a cake inside to bake.
  3. Avoid opening the oven door early so your cake rises evenly.
  4. Let baked cakes cool completely before slicing or serving.
  5. Use a sharp knife for cleaner and safer cutting.
