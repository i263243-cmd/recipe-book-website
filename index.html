Cooking Tips & Kitchen Basics

Pasta & Cooking Basics

  • Always add a pinch of salt to boiling water before adding pasta.
  • Keep a little pasta water aside to make sauces smooth and creamy.
  • Wash and dry fresh salad leaves thoroughly so dressing sticks well.

Baking Tips for Cakes

  1. Preheat your oven for 10 minutes before baking your cake.
  2. Measure flour, sugar, and cocoa powder accurately.
  3. Let the cake cool completely before slicing or serving.
