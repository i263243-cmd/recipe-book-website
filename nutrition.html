Recipe Calories Protein Carbs Fats
Chicken Pasta 480 kcal 32g 45g 18g

Healthy Ingredient Swaps

  • Use whole-wheat pasta instead of regular pasta for extra fiber.
  • Replace heavy cream or full-fat milk with low-fat milk in Chicken Pasta.
  • Swap regular sugar with honey or dark cocoa powder in Chocolate Cake.
